Open-Ended Project
I will build an inventory system designed to track and organize various categories of items. It will have a Program class, and an InventoryManager class to handle the list operations and saving and loading the inventory. To demonstrate inheritance and polymorphism, I will use a base class Item, with attributes like _itemName, _itemValue, and _itemQuantity. I will then have at least 5 derived classes like TechItem, ClothingItem, and ToolItem that will inherit from the base class. Each of the derived classes will add unique attributes and override a display method to format their specific details.
