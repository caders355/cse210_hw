Abstraction is when you take something complex, and strip it down to the core functionality and characteristic(s) (or multiple simpler ideas).
Abstraction can be incredibly helpful when starting a project. By breaking down the copmlexity into simpler 'components', it can be much easier to make desicions about the design of the program and the direction to take it. It can also help in making classes, functions, files, and everything needed for the program to function clean and clear. In my program, for example, I took the complexity of creating a journal entry, and made it so all the user has to do is decide to write, then actually write. Meanwhile, my program takes care of the other ideas (adding a date, selecting and including a prompt, etc.) in the background.
